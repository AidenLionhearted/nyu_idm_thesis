I built a tech demo for Demo Day.  That is, the mechanics for the game are in their own "Story" (game file).  This is so that the audience doesn't get distracted by the story/narrative and can focus on the technical aspects of the game.  There weren't many limitations since this is a tech demo.

Before showing the tech demo I'll be giving my presentation about the project.  It starts with a brief overview followed by explanations of my 3 research pillars, the story progression, and the game mechanics.  After the actual demo I'll go over my next steps for the project including some stretch goals.

I'm expecting that I'll be asked why I haven't put more interactivity into the game and why writing branching paths is a stretch goal.  I'll answer by addressing the scope of the project.  Also, that I would rather deliver a playable beta than an unfinished and unplayable game.

The questions I'd like to ask the audience are:

1) Is the text easy to read?
2) Is it obvious where to click for navigation and pickup up items?
3) Does the story make sense?
4) What, if anything, makes the game compelling?
