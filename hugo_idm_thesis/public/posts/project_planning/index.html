Weekly Plan

Week Date Due / In Class Game / Project Paper / Research
1 9/2 5-min project presentation Establish game concept, scope, tools, and resource needs. Establish research question and thesis direction.
2 9/9 First prototype; project journal; project plan Experiment with Twine, hosting, passages, links, and variables. Begin script. Identify research needs and potentially useful existing sources.
3 9/16 One-on-one with Vanessa Prepare 9/23 prototype. Continue script/Twine development and interaction experiments. Document design decisions/questions. Read/process ~2–3 sources. Review independent-study sources. Start annotated outline.
4 9/23 Prototype demo; related-work journal entry Use prototype feedback. Refine narrative structure, scope, major beats, and mechanics. Begin building toward Demo Day. Finish annotated outline. Continue research and identify how project relates to existing work.
5 9/30 Annotated outline due; Demo Day plan + 5-min presentation Build toward Demo Day. Implement narrative, interactions, mechanics, and UI needed for midterm. Draft Introduction, Research Question, and Hypothesis/guiding proposition. Continue research; identify gaps and organize sources by theme.
6 10/7 Demo Day prep; production-process journal Integrate narrative/mechanics. Resolve major technical problems. Test and stabilize Demo Day build. Begin Background/Lit Review. Refine earlier sections. Continue targeted research. Capture material for Methods/Process.
7 10/14 MIDTERM DEMO DAY Record feedback. Identify second-half development priorities. Continue toward complete playable game. Continue Background/Lit Review. Begin Methods/Process. Preserve Demo Day feedback for later analysis.
8 10/21 One-on-one; record Demo Day feedback; talk with relevant expert Develop from Demo Day feedback. Aim for complete beginning-to-end version. Begin formative testing when ready. Rough draft: ~6–12 pages; Background complete. Continue Methods/Process and targeted research.
9 10/28 Progress presentation; show paper to Vanessa Iterate from feedback/testing. Address story, pacing, navigation, UI, and technical issues. Move toward final version. Use Vanessa’s feedback. Expand/revise Intro, RQ, Hypothesis, Background, and Methods.
10 11/4 Documentation peer review; next-month project plan Finalize thesis-version game. Lock major content/design. Conduct final user testing and document results. Finish paper. Write Results and Discussion. Complete Abstract/Future. Revise, proofread, and check citations/formatting.
11 11/11 THESIS PAPER DUE; one-on-one; production work Continue production/polish based on testing and showcase priorities. Paper submitted. Shift to project documentation and presentation needs.
12 11/18 Production work; documentation peer review Continue production/polish toward showcase-ready version. Refine project documentation using peer/instructor feedback.
13 11/25 In-class Thesis Archive exercise Continue showcase preparation and artifact stabilization. Complete archive exercise in class; handle any necessary follow-up.
14 12/2 10-min final presentation; final deliverables / ProQuest Use presentation feedback for worthwhile final adjustments. Stabilize showcase build and rehearse demo. Complete remaining presentation/documentation requirements and prepare for showcase.
15 12/9 FINAL CRITS / PUBLIC SHOWCASE Present and demonstrate completed thesis game. Present research, process, results, and significance; complete remaining course responsibilities.
